## L298 decision and replacement path

**L298 suitability is HOLD, not proven FAIL.** One channel sees both brush currents summed; the other sees conveyor current. Obtain load/start/stall profiles at actual approved supply voltage, diode ratings, heatsink thermal path and simultaneous case temperature. The 2A/channel DC figure is an absolute maximum; a board's reliable continuous allowance may be substantially lower. At the datasheet's worst 2A bridge drop of 4.9V, loss can reach 9.8W in one channel. Calculate both channels together and the motor's delivered voltage. Lower software PWM does not qualify an overload. [ST L298 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/l298.pdf).

If the L298 fails, a practical **candidate** is two Pololu G2 18v17 #2991 boards. The manufacturer documents VIN, GND, OUTA, OUTB, PWM, DIR and SLP; its approximately 17A continuous claim depends on specified conditions and is not an approval for unknown loads. The default current-limit threshold is approximately 40A, so motor protection/VREF selection must be separately qualified. [Manufacturer product and pinout](https://www.pololu.com/product/2991).

Conditional revision only: GPIO21 -> brush PWM, GPIO22 -> brush DIR; GPIO23 -> conveyor PWM, GPIO25 -> conveyor DIR; gated EN -> both SLP inputs. Preserve GPIO14/27 and wheel/sensor pins. Remove/override each SLP pull-up according to the actual board revision with verified dominant default-low circuitry. VIN uses suitably split fused AUX supply; OUTA/OUTB replace OUT1/2 or OUT3/4; retain individual motor protection. Do not connect VM or 3V3(out) to external rails. Current/thermal and physical enable-disable qualification precede integration. Existing full-on forward-only auxiliary outputs could map HIGH PWM/LOW DIR, but adapter semantics, stop behavior, Wokwi labels, all schematics and regressions must be revised and reviewed before substitution. **This alternate topology is not the active E2 netlist and is not a ready-to-wire alternative.**
